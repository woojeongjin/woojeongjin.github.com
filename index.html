Woojeong Jin

Senior Applied Scientist · Hippocratic AI

I am a Senior Applied Scientist at Hippocratic AI, where I build real-time voice agents for healthcare that have handled 10M+ patient calls. My work spans speech and audio LLMs: contextual speech recognition, ASR error detection and recovery, and end-to-end speech-to-response models.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Southern California, advised by Prof. Xiang Ren. My doctoral research on multimodal learning and natural language processing appeared at ACL, EMNLP, and The Web Conference.

Before my Ph.D., I received a B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Seoul National University, where I worked on graph mining with Prof. U Kang.

Interests: Speech & Audio LLMsReal-time Voice AgentsSpeech RecognitionConversational AIMultimodal Learning
