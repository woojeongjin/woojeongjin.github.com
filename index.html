Woojeong Jin

Applied Scientist

I am an Applied Scientist at Hippocratic AI, where I focus on real-time voice agents.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Southern California, advised by Prof. Xiang Ren. During my doctoral studies, my research centered on multimodal learning and natural language processing.

Before pursuing my Ph.D., I received a B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Seoul National University, where I conducted undergraduate research under the guidance of Prof. U Kang.
